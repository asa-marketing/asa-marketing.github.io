Student Paper Awards

ASA Marketing Section

The ASA Marketing Section presents annual awards to recognize outstanding student research at the intersection of marketing and statistics. Award winners are invited to present their work at JSM.

2027 Call for Submissions

Doctoral Dissertation Research Award for 2027

We invite dissertation-based research papers from doctoral candidates worldwide working at the intersection of marketing and statistical methods.

Eligibility: Students expecting to graduate between September 2026 and August 2027.

Awards: The recipient receives $1,000 and a plaque. Finalists receive $250 and funding for JSM 2027 student registration, and are invited to present in Chicago, August 7–12, 2027.

Submission deadline: December 30, 2026 (11:59pm EST).

Submissions and questions: Prof. Jeff Dotson, dotson.83@osu.edu.
